# Monetization Strategy
## Coach-Centric Revenue Model

---

# 1. Core Philosophy

The platform is free and valuable for athletes.

Revenue primarily comes from coaches.

Analytics drives trust.
Marketplace drives revenue.

---

# 2. Revenue Streams

## 2.1 Commission Model (Primary)

Platform takes 15–25% commission on:

- Monthly coaching subscriptions
- Structured training programs
- One-on-one calls
- Diet plans
- Custom routines

---

## 2.2 Coach Pro Subscription (Recurring B2B SaaS)

Coaches pay ₹999–₹2999/month for:

- Advanced analytics dashboard
- Athlete risk flags
- Unlimited athlete management
- Custom plan builder
- Branded reports
- Revenue insights
- Priority listing in marketplace

This creates predictable recurring income.

---

## 2.3 Light Athlete Premium (Optional)

Small optional subscription for:

- Advanced downloadable reports
- Deep performance analytics
- CSV exports
- AI insights

Keep pricing low and non-restrictive.

---

# 3. Revenue Simulation

Example:

100 coaches
30 athletes per coach
₹2000/month average plan
20% commission

= ₹12,00,000/month commission revenue

Plus:
50 Pro coaches at ₹1500/month
= ₹75,000/month recurring

Total ≈ ₹12.75L/month

Without heavy user paywall.

---

# 4. Marketplace Strategy

Early Stage:
- 0% commission for first 3 months
- Free Pro tools beta
- Coach onboarding incentives

Mid Stage:
- Standard 20% commission
- Pro subscription introduced

Growth Stage:
- Corporate wellness packages
- Sponsored structured programs
- Affiliate partnerships

---

# 5. Key Business Metrics

- Coach Acquisition Rate
- Active Coaches
- Average Athletes per Coach
- Commission Revenue
- Coach Retention
- GMV (Gross Marketplace Volume)
- Platform Take Rate

---

# 6. Long-Term Revenue Expansion

- Corporate plans
- Running club dashboards
- White-label coach platform
- AI adaptive plan upsell
- Sponsored challenges
- Brand collaborations

---

# 7. Business Moat

Once coaches:

- Manage athletes here
- Store plans here
- Collect payments here
- Communicate here

Switching cost becomes high.

Data + Billing + Workflow = Lock-in.
